**BREAKING:** `AsyncProjectsClient.list()` now returns core's real contract — a flat `list[ProjectResponse]` — instead of the fabricated `{"data", "next_cursor", "has_more"}` envelope; the `cursor` param is gone (core paginates offset-based, so advance `offset` explicitly) and `sort_by`/`sort_dir` were added. `list_iter()` now offset-paginates and yields typed `ProjectResponse` models (the old envelope always carried `has_more=False`, so iteration silently stopped after page one). `list_members()` returns a flat `list[ProjectMemberResponse]` instead of the envelope dict; `limit`/`cursor` are gone (core's members endpoint is unpaginated and ignored them) in favour of `sort_by`/`sort_dir`.
